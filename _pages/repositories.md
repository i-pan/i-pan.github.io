## Kaggle competitions

Source code from my competition work, including team solutions hosted by collaborators.

### Pneumonia detection · 1st place

Detecting and localizing pneumonia on chest radiographs for the RSNA Pneumonia Detection Challenge.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/kaggle-rsna18) · [Solution write-up](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-pneumonia-detection-challenge/writeups/ian-pan-alexandre-cadrin-1st-place-solution-overvi)

### Pulmonary embolism detection · 2nd place

Detecting pulmonary emboli on CT using image models and sequence modeling for the RSNA STR competition.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/kaggle-rsna-pe) · [Solution write-up](https://www.kaggle.com/c/rsna-str-pulmonary-embolism-detection/discussion/193401)

### Melanoma classification · 2nd place

Classifying skin lesions for the SIIM-ISIC Melanoma Classification competition, using external training data and pseudo-labeling.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/kaggle-melanoma) · [Solution write-up](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/siim-isic-melanoma-classification/writeups/aloe-2nd-place-solution-overview)

### Breast cancer detection · 4th place

### Cervical spine fracture detection · 6th place

## Research and tools

### SKPv2 · Deep learning experimentation

My framework for rapidly prototyping deep learning experiments, with flexible Python configurations and a project template designed for Kaggle-style iteration.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/SKPv2)

### Pediatric bone age assessment

Hand localization and bone age estimation from radiographs, with training code, inference code, and pretrained models.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/boneage) · [Hugging Face model](https://huggingface.co/ianpan/bone-age) · [Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ianpan/bone-age-greulich-and-pyle)

### Active learning for diabetic retinopathy

Experiments studying how to select retinal photographs for labeling efficiently, accompanying our paper on deep active learning.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/diabetic-retinopathy-active-learning)

### Chest X-ray lines and tubes

A RANZCR-based project combining catheter and tube classification with segmentation of lines, endpoints, and the carina.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/cxr-lines-and-tubes) · [Hugging Face model](https://huggingface.co/ianpan/cxr-lines-and-tubes) · [Chest X-ray demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ianpan/chest-x-ray-ai)

### Head CT hemorrhage detection

Research models for detecting intracranial hemorrhage on head CT, with predictions at the slice and scan level and visualizations of hemorrhage location.

[Code](https://github.com/i-pan/ct-head-hemorrhage-detection) · [Hugging Face model](https://huggingface.co/ianpan/ct-head-hemorrhage-detection) · [Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ianpan/ct-head-hemorrhage-detection)

### Screening mammography

Models and a demo for breast cancer risk and breast density prediction from mammograms.

[Hugging Face model](https://huggingface.co/ianpan/mammoscreen) · [Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ianpan/screening-mammography)
